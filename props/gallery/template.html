Shared style

  • Flat normals on every mesh, five to twelve segments per curve, real-world units.
  • Lighting is a warm key from the front, a gold rim from the sun behind, and a plum fill from below.
  • Backdrop is a gradient sky shader, a sun disc, and four rings of faceted ridges with pines, faded into the haze by distance fog.
